## Newsletter Summary


This comparison explains when a retirement spreadsheet is enough and when retirement planning software is the better tool. Spreadsheets are flexible and familiar, but they can become fragile as taxes, healthcare, Social Security, Roth conversions, RMDs, survivor planning, scenarios, Monte Carlo, historical backtesting, and reports enter the picture. The AI Retirement Income Planner is positioned as a private, browser-based middle path for users who want spreadsheet-style ownership with purpose-built retirement logic.
