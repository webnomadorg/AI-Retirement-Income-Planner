## Facebook Post Snippets


**Snippet 1:**  
Rental income can help in retirement, but gross rent is not the number to plan from. Start with rent, then subtract mortgage, taxes, insurance, repairs, vacancy, management, and taxes.

**Snippet 2:**  
A rental property is different from a pension. It may provide income, but it can also bring repairs, tenant turnover, insurance increases, and management work.

**Snippet 3:**  
Before relying on rental income in retirement, test three scenarios: keep the property, sell it at retirement, and sell it later when management becomes harder.
