## Newsletter Summary


This step-by-step guide shows how to set up a retirement income plan from the first screen to a finished, saved file. It covers the five-phase model and why retirement income is best treated as a sequence rather than one averaged year, the two-step workflow of entering figures in Edit values and then fine-tuning on the Overview phase cards, the Quick Setup Wizard as the fastest start, how to read the all-important ending balance, live editing with instant tax and healthcare feedback, currency and residency options, the Replan from today workflow for annual course corrections, the built-in help and education, and how to save and export your work. The planner runs privately in your browser with no account or subscription.
