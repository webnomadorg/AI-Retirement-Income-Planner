# Promo material — How Healthcare Costs Move With Income: ACA and IRMAA

## Facebook Post Snippets


**Snippet 1:** Before 65, your health insurance cost can move with your income. Draw an extra $10,000 from a traditional IRA and you may lift MAGI by the full amount, shrinking your ACA subsidy. Draw it from cash or Roth and MAGI may not move at all. Same spending, very different healthcare bill.

**Snippet 2:** Here is the IRMAA twist that catches people out: the Medicare surcharge is usually based on your income from two years earlier. A Roth conversion at 63 can raise your Medicare premiums at 65. The tax is not the only cost of a big income year.

**Snippet 3:** A cheap health plan with a huge deductible is not always the cheapest plan. If your income qualifies you for cost-sharing reductions, a Silver plan can protect you far better. Before Medicare, healthcare planning is about total cost, not just the premium.

## Newsletter Summary


Healthcare costs move with income more than most plans admit. This article makes the case for reviewing healthcare inside the retirement income plan rather than bolting it on afterward. Before Medicare, ACA premium tax credits and cost-sharing reductions are tied to income, so the source of a withdrawal (cash, Roth, brokerage, or traditional) changes both taxes and the healthcare bill through MAGI. It explains the income-range question the ACA years pose, why cost-sharing reductions can matter as much as the premium, and the real tension between keeping income low for healthcare and using low-bracket space to defuse a future RMD. After 65 the question shifts to IRMAA, the Medicare surcharge that often uses income from two years earlier, so a conversion or capital gain today can raise premiums later. It covers one-time income events, why healthcare belongs in net income, and how expats face a different set of questions. Finally it shows how the planner surfaces ACA premiums and CSR bands before Medicare, Medicare Part B and D with IRMAA flags after, runs ACA, CSR, and IRMAA checks in the Plan Health Score, and documents every threshold on the Tax & ACA tab.
