## Facebook Post Snippets


**Snippet 1:**  
Can you retire with debt? Sometimes yes, but the type of debt matters. A fixed mortgage is different from credit card debt, a HELOC, or a tax bill. The important step is to model the payment, payoff date, taxes, healthcare, and survivor impact before setting the retirement date.

**Snippet 2:**  
Paying off debt before retirement can feel good, but the source of the payoff matters. If you use IRA or 401k money, taxes and healthcare income thresholds may change the real cost.

**Snippet 3:**  
Debt payments do not stop being important just because you retire. They become part of the retirement income plan. Test the plan with the debt, without the debt, and with a payoff strategy.
