## Facebook Post Snippets


**Snippet 1:**  
Your retirement income gap is the difference between what you need to spend and what reliable income covers. Once you know the gap, you can decide which accounts fill it.

**Snippet 2:**  
A monthly income gap should include taxes, healthcare, annual expenses, and one-time costs. Otherwise the plan may look better than it really is.

**Snippet 3:**  
The retirement income gap changes over time. It may be largest before Social Security starts, change again at Medicare, and change again when RMDs begin.
