## Newsletter Summary


Comparing scenarios is where retirement planning becomes genuinely useful, and this guide shows how. It explains the difference between the planner auto-saving your current work and saving a distinct version you can return to, why naming each scenario matters, and the two complementary ways to keep versions: up to three saved-plan slots in the browser for quick side-by-side comparison, and unlimited JSON exports saved to your own computer as a portable library and backup. It covers resetting to a clean baseline, comparing plans side by side either in the built-in compare view or across multiple browser tabs, what is actually worth comparing (part-time income and the ACA threshold, a downsizing lump sum, moving abroad, spending more or retiring earlier), and the optional "Ask AI about this comparison" feature. Everything stays on your own device.
