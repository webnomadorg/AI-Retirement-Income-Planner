Medicare IRMAA can raise Part B and Part D premiums when retirement income is higher. This article explains why Roth conversions, RMDs, capital gains, and large withdrawals, which Medicare reads two years later, should be modeled alongside Medicare costs, taxes, survivor planning, and long-term account balances.
