## Facebook Post Snippets


**Snippet 1:**  
"How much can I spend in retirement?" is not one number. It depends on income timing, taxes, healthcare, Social Security, RMDs, inflation, and how flexible your spending is.

**Snippet 2:**  
Before choosing a retirement spending number, test what happens if healthcare costs are higher, inflation lasts longer, or the market has a bad first decade.

**Snippet 3:**  
The 4 percent rule can be a starting point, but your real retirement spending plan needs taxes, healthcare, Social Security timing, RMDs, and survivor income built in.
