## Facebook Post Snippets


**Snippet 1:**  
Retiring at 60 is often less about one savings number and more about the five-year bridge before Medicare. Healthcare, taxes, withdrawals, and Social Security timing all need to fit together.

**Snippet 2:**  
At 60, you may be past many early withdrawal concerns, but you are usually still before Social Security and Medicare. That makes cash flow planning critical.

**Snippet 3:**  
Before retiring at 60, test three scenarios: retire now, work one more year, and work part time until 62. The difference may be larger than it first appears.
