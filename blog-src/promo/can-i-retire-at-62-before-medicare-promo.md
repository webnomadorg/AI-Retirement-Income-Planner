## Facebook Post Snippets

### Facebook Post 1

Can you retire at 62 before Medicare?

Maybe. But the real question is whether your bridge years work.

Before Medicare begins, you may need to model Marketplace coverage, Social Security timing, cash withdrawals, 401k withdrawals, taxes, and inflation together. A simple "Do I have enough saved?" calculator may miss the interaction.

### Facebook Post 2

Retiring at 62 creates a planning gap:

- Social Security may be available.
- Medicare usually is not.
- Healthcare costs may depend on income.
- 401k withdrawals may affect taxes.
- Cash can bridge the gap, but only if it lasts.

That is why I like modeling retirement in phases.

### Facebook Post 3

Bridge-year checklist before retiring at 62:

- Health insurance until 65
- Social Security at 62 vs 67 vs 70
- Cash bridge strategy
- 401k withdrawal strategy
- ACA income sensitivity
- Tax bracket headroom
- Inflation stress test
- Survivor income if married
