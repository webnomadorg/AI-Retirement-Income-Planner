## Newsletter Summary


MAGI stands for modified adjusted gross income. It usually starts with AGI and then adds back certain items for a specific tax rule or program. In retirement planning, MAGI can affect ACA Marketplace savings, Medicare IRMAA, Social Security taxation, Roth conversions, RMDs, withdrawal order, and taxable account sales. The planner's Tax & ACA tools help users compare MAGI-sensitive scenarios before verifying current rules with official sources.
