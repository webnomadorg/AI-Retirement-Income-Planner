## Newsletter Summary


The currency converter in the AI Retirement Income Planner translates amounts across USD, GBP, CAD, AUD, PHP, THB and EUR using the planner's live or custom rates, without touching your plan. Because the plan is kept in US dollars, a rate change alters the local-currency view rather than the results. The reliable workflow is to note the rate you plan with, enter each cost in the right field, then test currency risk by raising the dollar amounts for local costs and comparing that version with your base plan.
