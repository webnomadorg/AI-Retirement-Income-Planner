## Newsletter Summary


Before Medicare, a Silver ACA plan can unlock cost-sharing reductions (CSR), extra savings that lower your deductible, copays, and out-of-pocket maximum, often worth thousands of dollars a year. The catch is that CSR only attaches to Silver plans and only when household income is roughly 100% to 250% of the federal poverty level, in tiers (Silver 94, 87, and 73) that get stronger at lower incomes. The most costly mistake is qualifying for CSR and then buying a cheaper Bronze plan, which forfeits the benefit. CSR is a hard cliff at 250% FPL, so a year-end surprise can wipe it out. Early retirees have unusual control here, because Roth and cash withdrawals do not raise the MAGI figure that decides eligibility, though staying under the cliff can conflict with low-tax Roth conversions. This article explains how CSR works and shows how the planner surfaces the ACA Silver CSR status on every pre-Medicare phase, flags the cliff, and checks CSR headroom in Plan Health.
