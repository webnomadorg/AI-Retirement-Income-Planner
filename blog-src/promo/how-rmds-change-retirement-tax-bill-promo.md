## Newsletter Summary


Required minimum distributions can raise taxable income and affect more than federal tax. They may also influence Social Security taxation, Medicare IRMAA, state tax, survivor planning, and withdrawal choices. This article explains what RMDs are, why age 73 or 75 matters, and how to model RMDs before they begin.
