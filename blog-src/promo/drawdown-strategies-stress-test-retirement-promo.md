## Newsletter Summary


Real confidence in a retirement plan does not come from a single number; it comes from the same plan agreeing with itself across several independent tests. This guide shows how to use the planner's two validation tools. The Drawdown strategies tab runs your plan through six of the most tested withdrawal frameworks at once, the 4% rule, variable percentage withdrawal, the three-bucket approach, floor-and-upside, Guyton-Klinger guardrails, and Monte Carlo, all on your actual numbers, benchmarked against your plan's average monthly income. The stress test tab runs your plan across twelve combined return and inflation scenarios to check resilience. The article explains what each strategy tells you, how to read the "versus your plan" figure, why a guaranteed income floor makes everything look safer, and how to read all six together as convergent evidence. None of it predicts the future; it shows where a plan is fragile before that matters.
