## Facebook Post Snippets


**Snippet 1:**  
Retirement withdrawal guardrails are rules you set before a stressful year arrives. If markets fall, healthcare costs rise, or taxes spike, you already know what changes first.

**Snippet 2:**  
A strong retirement plan should include both spending cuts and spending increases. Guardrails can tell you when to tighten up and when the plan supports more freedom.

**Snippet 3:**  
Useful guardrail question: if your portfolio drops 15 percent in year one of retirement, what spending changes, what stays protected, and when do you review the plan again?
