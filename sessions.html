The concepts behind the numbers

Understand what the planner is modelling

To get the most from the planner it helps to know a few ideas it relies on. We explain what each one is and why the planner treats it the way it does — plain-English literacy, never a recommendation about your money.

MAGI

Modified Adjusted Gross Income — the figure that drives so many thresholds, and why the planner watches it closely.

ACA subsidies

How health-insurance subsidies work before Medicare, and the income "cliff" the planner helps you see coming.

CSR

Cost-sharing reductions — the extra Silver-plan help many people miss, and where it shows up in the plan.

IRMAA

The Medicare premium surcharges that kick in at higher incomes, and how the planner flags them.

RMDs

Required Minimum Distributions — when they start and why they matter for later-life tax.

Roth conversions

The basic mechanics of converting, and how the planner (and the Roth Optimizer) model the trade-offs.

Good to know

Session FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. A session is educational instruction — I teach you how the software works and what the concepts mean. It is not financial, tax, or legal advice, I don't give personalised recommendations, and I won't tell you what to do with your money. For advice tailored to your circumstances, speak to a licensed professional. Full details are in the session terms.

What if I want a second session?

At the end of your first session you'll be given a discount code for 50% off every future 1-on-1 session — so a follow-up hour is $60 rather than $120. There's no time limit and no obligation to use it; apply it at checkout whenever you'd like another session.

Do I need to buy the planner first?

Not necessarily. If you already own it, book the standalone 1-hour session. If you don't, the "1-hour session + the planner" option bundles the software in at a discount so you're ready to go.

What platform do we use?

Google Meet, running in your browser — the link is added to your calendar invite automatically. There's nothing to install and no account to create.

Do I have to share my real financial details?

No. We can work entirely with sample numbers, or with whatever figures you're comfortable showing on screen. Nothing sensitive is required, and I don't ask for account numbers, passwords or the like.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes — please give reasonable notice using the link in your confirmation email. The reschedule, cancellation and refund terms are set out on the session terms page.

What if none of the available times suit me?

Just email dev@webnomad.org with a few times that would work for you and I'll do my best to accommodate them. Once we agree a time that suits us both, you'll receive a calendar invitation by email with the Google Meet link.

What if I don't book a time right after paying?

That's fine. Your scheduling link is emailed to you and stays valid — pick a slot whenever it suits you. If you have any trouble, email dev@webnomad.org.
