About 150 words

The AI Retirement Income Planner is a retirement income tool built for the years when the arithmetic gets awkward: bridging to Medicare on taxable savings, deciding when to claim Social Security, and judging whether a Roth conversion is worth the tax it triggers.

It works phase by phase rather than as a single average, and it models the costs that usually get left out — federal and state income tax, the ACA subsidy cliff before 65, Medicare Part B and Part D afterwards, and the IRMAA surcharge that follows your income by two years. It also handles retiring abroad, with UK treaty treatment, Canadian and Australian tax, and expat healthcare costs.

Every calculation can be opened up and read step by step, so a figure can be checked rather than trusted. It is a single HTML file: one purchase, no subscription, no account, and nothing leaves the computer it runs on.

What makes it different

If you are comparing it against other retirement calculators, these are the honest points of difference rather than the marketing ones.

  • It shows its working. Every tax, subsidy and premium figure can be expanded into the steps that produced it — thresholds, rates, and which of your numbers went in. Most tools give you an answer and no way to check it.
  • It models healthcare properly. ACA subsidies and the 400% cliff before 65; Medicare Part B, Part D and all six IRMAA brackets afterwards, charged per person and using the two-year income lookback.
  • It works phase by phase. Retirement is not one long averaged year, and the interesting decisions live at the boundaries — the year Social Security starts, the year Medicare begins, the year a bucket runs dry.
  • You own it. One file, one payment. It keeps working if the developer stops, the website goes away, or the internet does.
  • It is built by one person who answers the support email himself.

What it is not

Worth stating plainly, because these are the things most often got wrong in coverage of tools like this:

  • It is not financial advice, and it is not a regulated advisory service. It is a calculator that models the scenarios you give it.
  • It is not a robo-adviser. It does not manage money, hold assets, connect to your accounts or place trades.
  • It is not a subscription and there is no account to create.
  • It does not send your data anywhere. The optional AI features connect to a provider only if you supply your own API key and choose to use them.
