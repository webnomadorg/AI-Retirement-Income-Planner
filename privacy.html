Privacy Policy

We built a planner that keeps your finances on your own device. This policy explains the little we collect on this website, why, and how to reach us about it.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

The short version: Your retirement plan never leaves your browser — there are no accounts, no bank links, and nothing about your numbers is sent to us. You don't have to take that on trust: every request the planner can make is listed here, and you can watch it yourself in your browser's Network tab.

This website is not the planner. The marketing site you are reading uses standard analytics, which stay off until you accept the cookie banner, and checkout is handled by Stripe under Stripe's own terms. The planner you download contains none of that — no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.

1. Who we are

This website and the AI Retirement Income Planner are made by Paul Hankin, an individual doing business as WebNomad Studio ("WebNomad Studio", "we", "us", "our"), based in Missouri, United States. Paul Hankin is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. If you have any question about privacy or your data, email dev@webnomad.org — a real person reads it.

This policy covers the website at this domain and the planner application you may purchase and download. It does not cover third‑party sites or services we link to, which have their own privacy policies (see Section 7).

2. The planner app: your data stays with you

The planner is a single, self‑contained file that runs entirely in your own browser. We want to be very clear about what this means for your privacy:

  • Nothing is uploaded to us. The figures you enter — income, savings, ages, scenarios — are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device, or in backup files you choose to export. We have no server that receives, stores, or sees your plan.
  • No account, no sign‑up. You don't create a login, and we don't hold a profile about you.
  • No bank or brokerage connections. The planner never links to your financial accounts.
  • Optional AI features run under your own key. If you choose to use the AI co‑pilot, it is called directly from your browser using an AI provider key that you supply. Those requests go from your browser to that provider under their privacy terms — they do not pass through us. The AI features are entirely optional and the planner works fully without them. What is sent is your plan, not your identity: the figures you entered and the results calculated from them, plus your question. The planner has no field for a name, email address, postal address, phone number, employer, or any account, policy or Social Security number, so none can be included; there is no login, so no user id or profile is attached; and your plan notes are excluded. The full detail of what does and does not leave your computer is set out here.
  • You're in control of your files. Exporting a JSON backup or a PDF report creates a file on your device. What you do with that file — where you store it or who you share it with — is up to you.

3. Information we collect on this website

This marketing website collects a small amount of information, in three ways:

a. When you contact us

If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, an optional subject, and your message. We use this only to reply to you and to keep a record of our correspondence. The message is delivered to us by email through our email‑sending provider, and a confirmation copy is sent back to your address. You can also email us directly instead of using the form.

b. When you request a free download

If you sign up for one of our free downloads — the eBook, a checklist or a guide — you provide your email address and, optionally, your first name. We use these to send you the download you asked for and occasional related emails about the planner. Your details are stored with our email marketing provider, and every email carries an unsubscribe link that removes you immediately. We never sell or rent the list.

c. Analytics and usage data

Like most websites, we use analytics tools to understand how visitors find and use the site — for example, which pages are popular and which features people are interested in. These tools collect technical and usage information such as your approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, the pages you view, and how you arrived. This data is aggregated and used to improve the site; we don't use it to identify you personally.

d. When you use site search

When you search this website, we record the words you searched for and how many results they returned. We do this for one reason: a search that finds nothing tells us we are missing a page people want, and it is the most useful signal we get for deciding what to write next.

We store only the search words and the number of results. We do not store your IP address, your browser, a cookie, or any identifier — there is nothing in these records that links two searches to the same person, or to you. Searches that look like they contain an email address or a long run of digits are discarded entirely rather than stored, in case something personal was typed into the box by mistake. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal, we record nothing at all.

e. Server and security logs

Our hosting provider automatically records standard technical information (such as IP address, timestamp, and requested page) for security, performance, and abuse‑prevention purposes.

4. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support.
  • To operate, secure, and maintain the website.
  • To understand traffic and improve our content and products.
  • To comply with legal obligations where they apply.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties except the service providers we rely on to run the site (listed below) or where required by law.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies for two purposes: to remember your display preferences (such as dark mode and your chosen color theme, which are stored locally in your browser), and — only with your consent — to power the analytics described above.

You're asked first. When you first visit, a cookie banner lets you accept or decline analytics cookies. Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel are not loaded unless you accept; if you decline, they never run. Your display preferences and other strictly necessary functions don't rely on consent. You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of any page.

You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers let you block them entirely — though some site preferences may not be remembered if you do.

One exception, stated plainly. When you deliberately submit a signup form for a free download, our own server tells Meta that a signup happened. We do this so we can tell which articles and adverts actually lead people to the download, and it happens whether or not you accepted the cookie banner — because it is triggered by an action you chose to take, not by browsing. What we send is the fact of the signup, your IP address, your browser type, and your email address only as an irreversible cryptographic hash, which lets Meta match the signup without us handing over the address itself. If — and only if — you accepted the cookie banner, we also include the two identifiers Meta's own pixel stores in your browser (which browser this is, and which advert you clicked to get here), so we can tell which advert actually led to a signup. If you declined the banner those identifiers don't exist and nothing takes their place. Simply reading this site still sends Meta nothing at all unless you accept the banner. If you would rather we did not do this, email dev@webnomad.org and we will stop and delete what we can.

6. Purchases are handled by Stripe

Checkout on this site is provided by Stripe, acting as the merchant of record for your purchase. Your payment is processed entirely by Stripe — we never see or handle your card details — and your transaction is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy. Stripe also calculates and remits any applicable sales tax or VAT, and emails your receipt. We only receive the information needed to fulfill and support your order (such as your email address and what you bought), and your download page verifies your purchase with Stripe each time it is used.

7. Third‑party services we use

To run this site we rely on a small number of reputable service providers, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Our hosting and analytics platform — serving the website and providing privacy‑oriented traffic analytics.
  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Conversions API — measuring the effectiveness of any advertising and understanding visitor interest, including the server‑side signup event described in section 5.
  • Our email delivery provider — delivering contact‑form messages and confirmations.
  • Our email marketing provider — storing the free‑download mailing list and sending the downloads and newsletter.
  • Stripe — payments, checkout, receipts and order fulfillment, as described above.

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own terms. We encourage you to review their policies if you'd like more detail.

8. How long we keep information

We keep contact‑form correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then delete it. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics providers' standard retention settings. You can ask us to delete correspondence you've sent us at any time.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over the personal information we hold about you — typically including the right to access it, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of analytics or marketing cookies. Because the planner stores your financial data only on your own device, you can exercise full control over that data yourself simply by editing, exporting, or clearing it in your browser.

To make a request about any personal information we hold (for example, contact‑form messages), email dev@webnomad.org and we'll respond.

10. Children's privacy

Our products and this website are intended for adults planning their finances. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, and we deliberately minimize what we collect. The strongest privacy protection in the planner is by design: your financial plan stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. No method of storage or transmission is ever completely secure, but keeping data local removes most of the risk.

12. Links to other websites

Our site links to external sites such as Stripe (our checkout) and our social media profiles. We're not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies before sharing information with them.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add a feature or change a service provider. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be made clear here.

14. Contact us

Questions, concerns, or requests about your privacy? Email dev@webnomad.org or use our contact form. We aim to reply within one business day.
